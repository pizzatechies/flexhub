How it works

From sign-up to paid in four steps

TenantFlex works for any number of complexes. Each one gets its own tenants, rates, bank details and invite code.

Register your complex

Management signs up in TenantFlex Manager, then sets the currency, billing day, electricity and water rates, and bank account.

Add units & tenants

Add your shops, offices and stalls. Tenants join with your invite code, or you create their accounts and assign units.

Bill automatically

Rent is billed on your billing day, meter readings become electricity and water bills, and one-off charges go out in seconds. Tenants are prompted instantly.

Get paid & receipted

Tenants pay in the app and receive a receipt immediately. Bank transfers wait for you to confirm, and cash is receipted at the office.

TenantFlex · for tenants

Your rent, utilities and receipts, all in your pocket

Tenants always know what they owe and why, and can settle it in a few taps.

  • Every bill and new charge: rent, electricity, water, service charges and penalties, with meter readings and rates shown.
  • Payment prompts: overdue bills and anything due within 7 days appear on the home screen with a Pay button.
  • Pay in the app: M-Pesa, Airtel Money, T-Kash, debit/credit card or bank transfer. Pay everything at once, or part of a bill.
  • Instant receipts: generated the moment a payment completes, then saved, downloadable as PDF and shareable.
  • Talk to management: chat about bills or repairs, and read notices from the complex.

Payments & receipts

Pay the way Kenya pays

Tenants choose how to pay, and each payment goes straight against their bills, oldest first.

M-Pesa Airtel Money T-Kash Debit / credit card Bank transfer Cash at the office
  • Mobile money & card: the tenant approves the prompt on their phone, and the receipt appears as soon as the payment is confirmed.
  • Bank transfer: the tenant sees your bank details and a reference, then submits their bank transaction code. You confirm it once it reaches the account, and the receipt is issued instantly.
  • Receipts that look the part: numbered per complex (RCT-001-2026-00042), itemised per bill, showing the remaining balance, as a PDF.
Billing automation

Bills that send themselves

Spend less time chasing money and more time running the complex.

  • Automatic monthly rent: every occupied unit is billed on your billing day, and never twice for the same month.
  • Meter readings → utility bills: enter this month's electricity or water readings and TenantFlex works out (current − previous) × rate for every unit.
  • One-off charges: service charges, penalties and repairs, sent to one tenant or to every tenant at once.
  • Reminders: tenants are reminded 3 days before a bill is due, and every week while it's overdue.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Which payment methods can tenants use?

M-Pesa, Airtel Money, T-Kash, debit/credit card and bank transfer in the app. Management can also record cash, cheques and bank deposits made at the office, with a receipt issued immediately.

How do live M-Pesa and card payments work?

Mobile money and card payments go through a payment-gateway connector. During setup you connect your own merchant account (for example M-Pesa Daraja / STK Push, Airtel Money or a card gateway), and money goes straight to your account. Bank transfers and office payments work without any gateway.

When does a tenant get their receipt?

The moment the payment completes: right after the mobile-money or card approval, as soon as management confirms a bank transfer, or immediately for cash recorded at the office. Receipts are saved in the app and can be downloaded as PDF.

Can we use a currency other than Kenyan Shillings?

Yes. KES is the default, and each complex can choose its own currency (USD, UGX, TZS, RWF and others) when it registers, or later in Settings.

Can one company run several complexes?

Yes. Each complex is registered separately with its own tenants, units, rates, bank account and invite code.

Do tenants have to install an app?

No. They can use the web version in any browser, or install the Android, iOS or Windows app. It's the same account on every device.

Who pays for TenantFlex?

The complex management pays one subscription per complex, monthly or annually (annual billing gets 2 months free). Tenants download and use the TenantFlex app for free.

Where is our data stored?

On your own TenantFlex server, in a PostgreSQL database. That can be an office PC, your own server or a cloud host.
