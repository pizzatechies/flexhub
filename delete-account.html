Delete your account

For TenantFlex and TenantFlex Manager · Pizza Technologies

You can delete your TenantFlex account yourself at any time, directly in the app.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open TenantFlex or TenantFlex Manager and sign in.
  2. Go to Account. On a phone it's under More → Account; in TenantFlex Manager it's at the bottom of Settings.
  3. Under Privacy & data, tap Delete my account.
  4. Enter your password and tap Delete account. You'll be signed out straight away.

Before deleting, you can tap Download my data to keep a copy.

Can't get into the app?

Contact us on info@pizzatechnologies.co.ke or WhatsApp +254 747 354 677 from the phone number or email address on your account, and tell us your complex's name. We'll confirm it's you and delete the account within the timelines required by the Kenya Data Protection Act. If you've only forgotten your password, your complex's management can reset it for you.

What is deleted, and what is kept

  • Deleted: your name, business name, email address, phone number, password, messages and notifications. Your unit is released.
  • Kept, without your name or contact details: bills, payments and receipts. Kenyan tax law requires the complex to keep payment records for at least five years, and they are deleted after that.
  • Deleting your account does not cancel money owed to your complex. Please settle balances with your complex's management.
  • Complex managers: the last manager of a complex can't delete their own account. Add another manager first, or contact us to close the complex's TenantFlex account.

More detail is in our Privacy Policy.
