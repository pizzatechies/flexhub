Data Processing Terms

Effective 29 October 2026

These Data Processing Terms form part of the agreement between Pizza Technologies ("Processor") and each complex that uses TenantFlex ("Controller"). They apply when Pizza Technologies processes personal data on the complex's behalf, as required by section 42 of the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019 (the "Act"). The person who registers a complex, or accepts these terms in TenantFlex Manager, confirms they are authorised to accept them for the complex.

1. Scope of processing

Subject matterHosting and operating TenantFlex for the Controller's complex.
DurationWhile the Controller uses TenantFlex, plus the return and deletion period in clause 8.
Nature and purposeStoring, organising, displaying and transmitting data to manage tenancies, issue bills, collect payments, issue receipts, and communicate with tenants.
Data subjectsThe Controller's tenants and their contact persons, and the Controller's staff who use TenantFlex Manager.
Personal dataNames, business names, email addresses, phone numbers, unit and tenancy details, bills and meter readings, payment records (including M-Pesa numbers and receipt numbers), messages and notifications.
Sensitive dataNone is required. The Controller should not enter sensitive personal data into TenantFlex.

2. Instructions

The Processor processes personal data only on the Controller's documented instructions. Those instructions are given by configuring and using TenantFlex and through these terms. The Processor also processes it where Kenyan law requires, in which case it will tell the Controller unless the law forbids it. The Processor will tell the Controller if it believes an instruction breaks the Act.

3. Confidentiality

Only authorised Pizza Technologies personnel who need it to provide or support TenantFlex can access the personal data, and they are bound by confidentiality.

4. Security

The Processor maintains appropriate technical and organisational measures (section 41 of the Act). These include:

  • encryption in transit (HTTPS)
  • password hashing, and encryption of integration credentials at rest
  • logical separation of each complex's data, and role-based access
  • sign-in rate limiting and restricted production access
  • backups provided by its hosting provider
  • regular review of these measures

5. Sub-processors

The Controller authorises the Processor to use sub-processors that host TenantFlex's servers and database (currently Render Services, Inc.). The Processor will bind each sub-processor to data protection obligations at least as protective as these, will remain responsible for them, and will give notice of new sub-processors so the Controller can object on reasonable grounds. Safaricom PLC processes M-Pesa payments as an independent controller under its own terms; it is not a sub-processor.

6. Transfers outside Kenya

Data may be hosted outside Kenya. The Processor ensures any such transfer complies with sections 48–50 of the Act and uses providers offering appropriate safeguards.

7. Assistance

  • Data subject requests: TenantFlex lets tenants export and delete their own data. The Processor will help the Controller answer other requests under sections 26 to 40 of the Act, and will pass on any request it receives directly.
  • Breaches: the Processor will notify the Controller without undue delay, and in any case within 48 hours, of becoming aware of a personal data breach. It will provide the information needed for the Controller's notification to the Data Commissioner under section 43 of the Act.
  • The Processor will reasonably help with data protection impact assessments and with enquiries from the Data Commissioner.

8. Return and deletion

When the Controller stops using TenantFlex, it can export its data for 30 days. After that, the Processor deletes the Controller's personal data, except where Kenyan law requires it to be retained. Such records are kept only as long as required, and protected.

9. Information and audits

The Processor will make available the information reasonably needed to show compliance with these terms, and will answer the Controller's reasonable written questions. On-site audits require reasonable notice and must respect the confidentiality and security of other customers.

10. Controller's responsibilities

The Controller confirms it has a lawful basis for the personal data it puts into TenantFlex. It will inform its tenants how their data is used (it may refer them to the TenantFlex Privacy Policy) and keep its data accurate. Where the Act requires, it will be registered with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner.
