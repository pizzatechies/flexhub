Pricing FAQ

About your subscription

What's the difference between monthly and annual?

Same features. Monthly is billed every month. Annual is paid once a year and costs the same as 10 months, so you get 2 months free.

Do tenants pay anything?

No. The subscription is paid by the complex management, and tenants use the TenantFlex app for free.

What if we have more units than our plan allows?

Move up to the next plan at any time. Enterprise has no unit limit and covers several complexes.

Are payment-provider fees included?

No. M-Pesa, Airtel Money, T-Kash and card transaction fees are charged by the provider under your merchant account. Bank transfers and cash have no TenantFlex fees.
