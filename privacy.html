Privacy Policy

Effective 29 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Pizza Technologies ("we", "us"), a company registered in Kenya, handles personal data in TenantFlex: the TenantFlex tenant app, the TenantFlex Manager app, their web versions, the TenantFlex servers we operate, and this website. It is written to meet the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019 (the "Act") and its regulations.

1. Who is responsible for your data

  • Tenant data inside a complex. Each business or shopping complex ("the complex") that uses TenantFlex decides what tenant data to collect and why, such as bills, payments, units and messages. For that data the complex is the data controller and Pizza Technologies is its data processor: we process it only on the complex's instructions, under our Data Processing Terms. For questions about your tenancy records, contact your complex's management first. You can also contact us and we will help.
  • Everything else. For management accounts, subscriptions and billing of complexes, support enquiries, and this website, Pizza Technologies is the data controller.

3. How we use it, and our lawful basis

  • To provide TenantFlex: showing bills, taking payments, issuing receipts, delivering messages and notifications, and running management dashboards. Basis: performance of a contract (s.30(1)(b)(i) of the Act), on behalf of your complex.
  • To process payments: sending the amount and your M-Pesa number to Safaricom so you get the PIN prompt, then recording Safaricom's confirmation. Basis: contract.
  • To keep records required by law: financial and tax records. Basis: legal obligation.
  • To keep TenantFlex secure: preventing fraud and unauthorised access, and fixing faults. Basis: legitimate interests.
  • To support and bill complexes: answering enquiries and invoicing subscriptions. Basis: contract and legitimate interests.

We do not sell personal data, use it for advertising, or use it to make automated decisions with legal effects on you.

4. Who we share it with

  • Your complex's management sees the data of its own tenants. Other complexes never see it.
  • Safaricom PLC receives the phone number and amount needed to process M-Pesa payments, and handles them under its own privacy statement.
  • Hosting providers that run our servers and database (for example Render Services, Inc.) act as our sub-processors under contracts that require them to protect the data.
  • Banks: when you pay by bank transfer, your reference is shared with the complex so it can match the payment.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it, such as a court order or a tax or regulatory request.

5. Transfers outside Kenya

Our servers may be hosted outside Kenya, for example in the United States or the European Union. Where data is transferred outside Kenya, we do so in line with sections 48–50 of the Act. We use providers that give appropriate safeguards for the security and protection of personal data, and we transfer only what is needed to run TenantFlex.

6. How long we keep it

  • Account details: while the account is active. When you delete your account they are erased (see Delete your account).
  • Bills, payments and receipts: kept for at least five years after the transaction, as Kenyan tax law requires business records to be kept. After an account is deleted, these records are kept without the person's name and contact details.
  • Messages and notifications: until the account is deleted or the complex closes its TenantFlex account.
  • Technical logs: kept briefly, only for security and troubleshooting.
  • When a complex leaves TenantFlex, its data is returned or deleted as set out in the Data Processing Terms, except records we must keep by law.

7. How we protect it

All connections are encrypted (HTTPS). Passwords are stored as one-way scrypt hashes, and M-Pesa credentials are encrypted with AES-256-GCM. Each complex's data is kept separate from every other complex, and tenants can see only their own records. Sign-in attempts are rate-limited, and access to production systems is restricted to authorised Pizza Technologies staff. No system is perfectly secure. If a personal data breach occurs, we will notify the affected complex, the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner and, where required, you, as set out in section 43 of the Act.

8. Your rights

Under the Act you have the right to:

  • be informed about how your data is used (this policy)
  • access your data. Use Account → Privacy & data → Download my data in the app, or ask us
  • correct inaccurate data. Edit your profile in the app, or ask your complex to correct bills
  • delete your data. Use Account → Delete my account, or see Delete your account
  • object to processing, or withdraw consent where processing relies on consent
  • data portability: your export is a machine-readable JSON file

To exercise a right, contact info@pizzatechnologies.co.ke or WhatsApp +254 747 354 677. We may need to verify your identity, and we respond within the timelines set by the Data Protection (General) Regulations, 2021. You can also complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (www.odpc.go.ke).

9. On your device and on this website

The apps store only what they need to work on your device: your sign-in token and the address of your TenantFlex server. This website uses no cookies, analytics or advertising. It is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts, which receive your IP address to deliver those files. The live demos on this website run entirely in your browser with sample data: nothing you do in a demo is sent to us.

10. Children

TenantFlex is for businesses and adults. It is not directed at children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

11. Changes

We will post any changes here with a new effective date. If changes are significant, we will tell you in the app and ask you to accept them again.
