What happens next

From first message to first payment

  1. Tell us about your complex. The number of units, how you bill rent and utilities today, and how tenants pay.
  2. See a demo. We walk you through TenantFlex and TenantFlex Manager using a sample complex.
  3. We set it up. We set up your TenantFlex server and your payment options (M-Pesa, Airtel Money, T-Kash, card, bank transfer), then install the apps for your management team.
  4. Invite your tenants. Tenants get the TenantFlex app on Android, iPhone, Windows or the web, and join with your complex's invite code.
  5. Start billing. Rent, electricity and water bills go out, tenants pay in the app, and receipts are issued instantly.
